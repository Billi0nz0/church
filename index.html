OUR BELIEFS

Statement of Faith

Our hope is found in Jesus Christ alone, the Savior who gives life, peace, and eternal redemption. Through God's Word we are guided, strengthened, and transformed to live according to His will.

The Trinity

We believe in one God eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Holy Spirit

We believe in the power of the Holy Spirit to transform lives, guide believers in truth, and empower God's people.

Salvation

We believe salvation comes through Jesus Christ, whose death and resurrection secured our redemption.

God's Word

We believe the Holy Bible is God's inspired Word and the foundation of our faith and practice.
